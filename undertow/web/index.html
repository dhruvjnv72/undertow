Cascade simulator: what could a forced chain reaction do?

Pick a price. Undertow finds every position that would be force-closed on the way there, then pushes those forced orders through the real order book to see how far they could move the price. It is a worst-case estimate, not a forecast.
1 · Target price: where could the price go?
2 · How much of the forced orders reach the order book?
3 · Chain reaction between rounds
Ticked: after each round the price is assumed to move to where the book pushed it, which can liquidate more positions in the next round. Unticked: only the first round is simulated. Perpl's real mark price follows the oracle, so ticked is a stress assumption.

Funding overview

Perpl settles funding every 8,571 blocks, about 43 minutes. A positive rate means longs pay shorts. “Per year” is the rate times the number of intervals in a year (about 12,200), a rough guide because the rate changes.
